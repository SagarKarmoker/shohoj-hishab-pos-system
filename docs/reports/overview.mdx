# Reports Overview

Shohoj Hishab provides reports to help you understand sales and profitability.

## Available reports

- **Daily sales summary** — Delivered via SMS at closing time (e.g., 9 PM)
- **Top selling products** — Best performers for the week
- **Profit margin** — Profit after cost deduction
- **Seasonal trends** — Alerts and comparisons (e.g., "You sold 40% more umbrellas last July")

:::info
Report screens and filters will be documented as they are implemented.
:::
