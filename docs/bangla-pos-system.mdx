# Shohoj Hishab (সহজ হিসাব) - Bangla POS System for Small Retailers

## Overview

A mobile-first Point of Sale system designed specifically for Bangladeshi small retailers (kirana stores, cosmetics shops, mobile accessories, clothing boutiques).

**Target Users:**
- Shop owners with 10L-50L BDT annual revenue
- Currently using pen-paper or mental accounting
- ~1.5 million small retail shops in Bangladesh

---

## Core Features

### 1. Ultra-Simple Billing
- One-tap item selection with icons (not just text)
- Voice input: Speak "2 kg rice, 1 liter oil" → auto-adds to bill
- Photo capture of items for inventory (AI recognizes products)
- Split payment: Cash + bKash in single transaction
- SMS receipt (no printer needed) or thermal Bluetooth printer support

### 2. Smart Inventory
- Auto-deduct stock on every sale
- Low stock alerts via SMS
- Expiry date tracking for medicines/cosmetics
- Supplier contact integration (call supplier directly from app)
- Purchase order generation when stock hits threshold

### 3. bKash/Nagad/Rocket Integration
- Generate payment QR codes instantly
- Auto-reconcile payments (matches bKash confirmation with sale)
- Credit/udhar tracking (customers who buy on tab)
- Automated reminder SMS for pending payments

### 4. Business Intelligence
- Daily sales summary at 9 PM via SMS
- Top selling products this week
- Profit margin calculator (shows profit after cost deduction)
- Seasonal trend alerts ("You sold 40% more umbrellas last July")

### 5. Staff Management
- Multiple user accounts (owner, manager, salesman)
- Permission levels (salesman can only sell, owner sees all data)
- Attendance tracking with selfie clock-in
- Commission calculation for sales staff

### 6. Offline-First Design
- Works without internet during power outages
- Auto-syncs when connection returns
- Data backup to cloud daily

## Technical Considerations

- **Platform**: Android-first (Java/Kotlin or Flutter)
- **Offline Storage**: SQLite with sync queue
- **Cloud**: AWS Mumbai or local Bangladesh server for data sovereignty
- **Security**: End-to-end encryption for financial data
- **Integrations**: bKash API, Nagad API, SMS gateway (e.g., SSL Wireless)

---

## Risk Factors

1. **Adoption**: Shop owners may resist change from familiar paper methods
2. **Connectivity**: Internet availability varies by area
3. **Competition**: Larger players (ShopUp, Pathao) may enter
4. **Hardware**: Printers/scanners need reliable supply chain
5. **Support**: Need Bangla-speaking support team for troubleshooting
